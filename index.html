Product Management · Capstone · 2026

NairaNav

Navigate Your Naira. Drive Your Future.

A mobile financial companion designed exclusively for ride-hailing drivers in Nigeria — turning income unpredictability into financial clarity and control.

Fintech Mobile Nigeria MoSCoW User Research PRD

The Problem

Ride-hailing drivers on Bolt, Uber, and InDrive face severe income unpredictability — no tools to forecast earnings, protect savings, or understand platform fees. Millions of Nigerian households depend on this income with no financial safety net.

The Solution

NairaNav aggregates earnings across platforms, forecasts weekly income using AI-driven pattern analysis, automates micro-savings, and alerts drivers before they fall below their income targets.

MVP Features

Earnings Dashboard
AI Income Forecast
Auto-Save Wallet
Low Income Alerts
Fuel Cost Tracker
Trip Performance Insights

Methodology

End-to-end PRD · User persona research · MoSCoW prioritisation · Stakeholder mapping · 3-phase launch plan (private beta → soft launch → scale) · Metrics defined with DAU/MAU, forecast accuracy, and churn targets
